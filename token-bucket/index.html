Challenge 1

Keep CAPACITY = 10 and slow the refill down: try REFILL_EVERY = 30, then 60. Roughly how slow must refills be before the bot can't beat 10 in a minute?

Show the answer

About one token a minute (60 s). A full bucket already spends the whole minute's allowance, so the worst minute is CAPACITY plus every refill that lands inside it (at 30 s you still get 11). To allow bursts and still enforce the limit, shrink the bucket instead: CAPACITY = 5 with REFILL_EVERY = 12 lets 9 through in the worst minute.

Challenge 2

Predict first: CAPACITY = 1, REFILL_EVERY = 6. How many get through in the worst minute? And who pays for it?

Show the answer

9, so the limit holds, but there's no burst left at all: anyone who taps twice within 6 seconds gets a 429, like a fan holding three seats for friends. Capacity sets how friendly the limiter is; the refill rate sets how strict it is.
